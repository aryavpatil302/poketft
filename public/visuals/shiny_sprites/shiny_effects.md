This is a list of all the Pokemon and their shiny effects - these effects are applied when a shiny Pokemon is fielded.

Shiny Jungle Pokemon

Tangela: Grants all allies a 75-Health shield at the start of combat.

Ribombee: Pollen Puff heals and deals damage 1.5x as effectively.

Venusaur: Grants allies in the front 2 rows +100 max Health.

Vigoroth: Grants all allies +10% Attack Speed at the start of combat.

Vikavolt: Discharge deals 33% bonus damage to the unit with the highest current Health in the targeted row.

Toucannon: Applies burn to all enemies for the first 15 seconds of combat.

Tropius: Grants all allies +100 max Health.

Tapu Bulu: Regenerates 1% of max Health per second.


Shiny Beachy Pokemon

Kingler: Grants all allies +5 Attack.

A-Raichu: Grants all allies +10 Special Attack.

Palossand: Starts combat with a 200-Health shield and +10 Defense and Sp. Defense.

A-Exeggutor: The egg bounces one extra time, dealing 25% damage on the extra bounce.

Blastoise: Starts combat with +30% Attack Speed.

Tapu Fini: Whirlpools execute enemies below 10% Health.


Shiny Volcano Pokemon

Typhlosion: Eruption applies a 5-second burn to each enemy hit.

Graveler: Grants the whole team +5 Defense.

Torkoal: Starts combat with +20 Defense.

Gible: Bite reduces the target's durability (Defense and Sp. Defense) by 5%.

Armarouge: Gains +15 Attack and +15% Attack Speed.

A-Marowak: Grants all allies +20 Attack.

Wheezing: At the start of combat, sunders and shreds the entire enemy team for 15 seconds.

Charizard: Has 30 less base Mana.


Shiny Sky Strikers Pokemon

Pidgeotto: Starts combat with +15 Attack.

Wailord: Bounce damage is tripled.

Talonflame: Heals for 75% of the damage dealt if Brave Bird kills its target.

Noivern: Grants all allies +20 Special Attack.

Rayquaza: Starts combat with full Mana and a 10% damage amp.


Shiny Cave Crawlers Pokemon

Zubat: Poison damage is doubled.

Druddigon: Grants allies in the same row +10 Attack.

Sableye: Each cast has a 30% chance to grant 1 Gold.

Ferrothorn: Heals for 100% of the damage an enemy takes from Iron Barbs.

Excadrill: Grants all allies +15% critical strike chance.


Shiny River Pokemon

Quagsire: Grants all allies +20 Sp. Defense.

Shiny Temporal Woods Pokemon

Shiny Ruiner Pokemon

Sneasler: Ally attacks poison enemies for the first 10 seconds of combat.
